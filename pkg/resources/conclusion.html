One step is left, and it is yours.

vhid types and clicks through a driver extension, and macOS will not turn a driver extension on until the person at this Mac says so. No installer can do that for you. Until you do, vhid cannot type or click.

  1. Open System Settings > General > Login Items & Extensions.
  2. Click the (i) beside Driver Extensions.
  3. Turn on org.pqrs.Karabiner-DriverKit-VirtualHIDDevice, and authenticate when asked.

If macOS showed an alert saying a system extension was blocked, this is the same switch.

To check, run vhid driver state in Terminal. It prints running once the driver is on. If it prints installed-inactive, macOS never received the request, which happens when nobody was logged in during the install. Run this installer again while you are logged in, then turn the switch on.
