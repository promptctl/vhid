One step is left, and it is yours.

vhid types and clicks through a driver extension, and macOS will not turn a driver extension on until the person at this Mac says so. No installer can do that for you. Until you do, vhid cannot type or click.

If System Settings opened at Driver Extensions, turn on org.pqrs.Karabiner-DriverKit-VirtualHIDDevice there, and authenticate when asked. Otherwise open System Settings > General > Login Items & Extensions, click the (i) beside Driver Extensions, and turn on the same switch. If macOS showed an alert saying a system extension was blocked, this is the same switch.

Then run vhid doctor in Terminal. It prints ready once vhid can type and click, and otherwise names the one step left. If it says the activation request never landed, which happens when nobody was logged in during the install, run this installer again while you are logged in, then turn the switch on.
